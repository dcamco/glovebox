QuickJackFront block: frame directly under the angled (oblique) chassis tube just behind each front wheel, the same as the lift-point diagram. Rear block: on the matching angled tube just ahead of each rear wheel, but further outboard, so both rails stay parallel. Rails must be parallel because the arms lift in an arc and the car shifts along its length. Don't open the doors while it's lifted: the body flexes enough that they won't close until it's back down.FerrariChat, 308/328 QuickJack 5000 EXT (2020): thread. Short rails leave the engine end unsupported, so owners recommend the EXT (long) version. Alternative: turn the units crosswise under the car.
