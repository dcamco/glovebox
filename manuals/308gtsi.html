Open

Coolant loss at the water-pump end of the engine

Coolant level was low with the engine cold. After topping up, coolant collected beneath the water-pump area.

Likely sources, in order: hose clamps relaxed on the September 2024 hoses; pump shaft seal at the weep hole; pump gasket or housing O-ring; spillage during filling.

Next step: access through the right-rear wheel arch, clean and dry the area, and pressure-test cold to the rating stamped on the cap. The cam belts share this end of the engine and should be checked for contamination. The pump and hoses are Moorespeed's September 2024 work; they should be consulted if the pump is implicated.
